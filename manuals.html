Manuals & Apps

Treatment guides, written to be used.

Manuals, protocols and small applications for working clinicians — built by someone who has to use them on Monday morning.

What this is

Practical, not academic.

Most treatment manuals are written for trials. They describe a protocol precisely enough to be studied and not clearly enough to be used at four o'clock on a Thursday.

The work here goes the other way: what to listen for, what to say, what to do when the session doesn't go where the model says it should. Structured enough to teach, honest enough about where the structure runs out.

The first is a pragmatic psychodynamic manual — defense, affect, attachment, and the transference as they actually show up in a general practice caseload.

How these are built. Where something is delivered as an application rather than a document, it runs client-side only: nothing you or a client enters is transmitted or stored. It stays on your screen until you print it or write it into your own record.
