Coaching

Depth. Threshold. Return.

One-to-one coaching for people at a turning point — in work, in a relationship, in what your life means. Not therapy. A conversation that goes to the root of it.

Who this is for

People at a turning point.

Most people come here at a turning point. A job that no longer fits. A relationship that ended, or needs to. A ceremony that opened something you haven't been able to put down. A life that looks right and doesn't feel right.

You are not in crisis. You are functioning. And you know the question you're carrying is larger than a technique will answer.

That's the work.

What happens in a session

Sixty minutes, by video.

You bring what's alive right now. A decision. A pattern you keep repeating. A question you can't set down.

I listen for what's underneath it. Then I say what I see, plainly, and ask the questions that move it. When direction helps, I give it.

You leave with something you can actually do before we meet again. The work between sessions is yours. The sessions are where it gets seen clearly.

How this differs from therapy

This is coaching, not treatment.

Therapy treats conditions. It works through diagnosis, often through insurance, toward relief and healing.

Coaching does something else. No diagnosis. No clinical record. No insurance. The focus is direction: what's true for you now, and what you do about it.

Therapy tends what was hurt. This work is about who you're becoming.

I'm clinically trained. That matters for one reason: if what comes up needs treatment, I'll tell you, and help you find it.
