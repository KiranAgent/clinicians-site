Tools

Structured tools for the room.

Frameworks that organize a conversation and surface pattern — built for clinicians who think structurally about attachment, defense, and relational shape.

What these are

Tools, not tests.

These are structured clinical tools: they organize what you're already listening for and make a pattern visible to both people in the room.

They are not validated psychometric instruments. They produce no normed score, no cut-off, and no diagnosis. Used well, they do what a good genogram or a values sort does — give a conversation a spine.

Each is built to be used inside your own clinical judgment, not in place of it.

How they're built. Client-side only. Nothing you or a client enters is transmitted or stored anywhere — the work happens in your browser and stays on your screen until you print it or write it into your own record.
